Mercante in Fiera

A classic Italian card game, reborn on your iPhone

Welcome

Mercante in Fiera brings the charm of the traditional Italian fair card game to your iPhone. Whether you're playing solo against a smart AI or gathering friends around a single device, every round delivers the same suspense, bluffs, and laughter that have made this game a beloved pastime for generations.

Need a hand? Our support team is ready to help with anything from setup questions to feedback. Reach us anytime at dhfu94086@gmail.com.

What's Inside

  • Authentic Mercante in Fiera rules, faithfully recreated for touch screens
  • Play against up to three AI opponents with adjustable difficulty
  • Pass-and-play local multiplayer for gatherings with friends and family
  • Customisable player profiles with avatars from your photo library
  • Achievement system to track milestones and rare wins
  • Dark, light, and system-following interface themes
  • Haptic feedback and sound effects for an immersive feel
