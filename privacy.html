Privacy Policy

Mercante in Fiera

Last Updated: October 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Mercante in Fiera ("we," "our," or "us") collects, uses, and safeguards your information when you use our mobile application (the "App"). By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

Short version: Mercante in Fiera is designed to keep your data on your device. We don't require an account, we don't run ads, and we don't sell your information. You stay in control.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide

Mercante in Fiera does not require you to create an account or provide personal information to play. All gameplay data is stored locally on your device.

If you choose to personalize your merchant profile, you may optionally:

  • Select a portrait photo from your device's photo library
  • Capture a profile photo using your device's camera
  • Enter a display name for your in-game merchant

This information remains on your device unless you explicitly export or share it.

1.2 Automatically Collected Information

Like most mobile apps, the App may automatically collect limited technical information such as:

  • Device model and operating system version
  • App version
  • Crash logs and performance diagnostics (provided only with your consent)
  • Anonymous usage analytics (provided only with your consent)

2. Permissions We Request

The App only requests the following iOS permissions, and each is asked only when needed:

  • Camera (NSCameraUsageDescription): Used solely to capture a portrait for your merchant profile. Photos you take are stored locally; we never upload them.
  • Photo Library (NSPhotoLibraryUsageDescription): Used to let you pick a portrait from your library and to save souvenir cards or victory artwork you choose to export.
  • Photo Library Additions (NSPhotoLibraryAddUsageDescription): Used only when you tap "Save" on a souvenir card or victory artwork.

You can revoke these permissions at any time in your device's Settings app.

3. How We Use Your Information

Any information collected or generated is used only to:

  • Provide and improve the App's features and stability
  • Remember your settings, achievements, and progress
  • Diagnose crashes and fix bugs
  • Respond to your support requests when you contact us

We do not use your information for advertising, profiling, or any form of automated decision-making that produces legal effects.

4. Data Storage and Security

All gameplay data, settings, profile data, and achievements are stored locally on your device using standard iOS sandbox storage. We apply industry-standard safeguards and rely on Apple's built-in device security.

Because data is stored on your device, deleting the App will permanently remove all associated data.

5. Third-Party Services

Mercante in Fiera does not integrate third-party advertising, social, or analytics SDKs by default. If we ever introduce optional third-party services (e.g., optional analytics), we will update this policy and ask for your consent where required.

Apple may collect certain diagnostic data independently under their own privacy terms. For details, please review Apple's Privacy Policy.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is suitable for general audiences and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction). Because we do not collect information online, the App can be used by children with appropriate parental supervision.

7. Your Rights

Depending on where you live (for example, in the EEA, the UK, or California), you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or export your personal data. Because all data lives on your device, you can exercise most of these rights yourself by deleting the App or adjusting settings. For anything else, contact us.

8. International Data Transfers

We do not transfer your personal data to our servers or to any third party. Any email correspondence you initiate with us is handled using standard email providers; please review those providers' policies for details on data handling and retention.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page. Material changes will be communicated through the App where appropriate.
