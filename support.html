Frequently Asked Questions

Q. How do I start a new game?
Open the app, choose Play from the home screen, then pick your mode: Solo vs AI, or local Pass-and-Play with friends.
Q. Where is my progress saved?
Everything is saved locally on your device. If you uninstall and reinstall the app, your saved data will be removed.
Q. Can I play with friends online?
The current version supports local Pass-and-Play on a single device and Solo vs AI modes. Online multiplayer is not available yet.
Q. How do I change my profile picture?
Go to Profile, tap your avatar, and choose either "Take Photo" or "Choose from Library." You can revoke the camera or photo permission at any time from iOS Settings.
Q. Can I reset my achievements or stats?
Yes. Head to Settings → Reset Progress to clear all local data. This action cannot be undone.
Q. The app feels laggy or crashes. What should I do?
Please make sure your iPhone is running the latest version of iOS and the App Store version of Mercante in Fiera. If the issue persists, restart the device and email us with details.
Q. Does the app contain ads or track me?
No. Mercante in Fiera contains no ads and no third-party tracking. See our Privacy Policy for details.
Q. Will the rules ever change?
We aim to faithfully preserve the traditional Mercante in Fiera rules. Any rule changes will be clearly documented in the in-app rulebook and release notes.
